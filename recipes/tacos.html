Simple Beef Tacos

Description

Quick and eady beef tacos with fresh toppings and plenty of flavour.

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon chilli powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • 8 taco shells
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ lettuce
  • 75 grated cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Finely chop the onion and garlic
  2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the onion and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add the beef mince and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon.
  5. Add the garlic, tomato paste, cumin, paprika, and chilli power. Stir well.
  6. Season with salt and pepper and cook for another 2-3 minutes.
  7. Warm the taco shells according to the packet instructions.
  8. Fill each shell with the beef mixture.
  9. Top with lettuce, tomato and grated cheese.
  10. Serve immediately.
