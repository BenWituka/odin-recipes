Pancakes

Description

A simple, moist banana bread made with ripe bananas. It's a great way to use up bananas that are past their best.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 100g melted butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 200g plain flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl.
  3. Stir in the melted butter, brown sugar, egg, and vanilla extract.
  4. Add the baking soda and salt, then stir.
  5. Gradually add the flour and mix until just combined. Don't overmix.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased loaf tin.
  7. Bake for 50-60 minutes, until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  8. Allow the banan bread to cool for 10 minutes before removing it from the tin.
